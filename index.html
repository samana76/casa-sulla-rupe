This stand-alone house sits on the Western cliff of Orvieto, free on all four sides. It commands truly stunning panoramic views with breathtaking sunsets, and offers absolute privacy — while being just steps from restaurants, cafés, piazzas, and monuments.


Just 50 metres from the 11th-Century Church of San Giovenale, and a leisurely 10-minute walk from the magnificent Orvieto Cathedral. This is the most coveted panoramic location in the city.


A home in perfect condition, ready to be lived in from day one — or offered for short-term rental on Airbnb and VRBO, as it currently operates.

Ancient Heritage

The Etruscan
Quarry

"Beneath this floor, a large square quarry cut by the Etruscans reaches all the way to the bottom of the cliff."

Part of the quarry, along with several ancient cisterns, have been emptied, illuminated, and made fully accessible. The tuff walls throughout have been hand-chiseled and treated with a breathable fixative, preserving their raw historic character.

The restoration and excavation work was carried out following strict historical criteria, ensuring that the ancient fabric of the building is honored in every detail.
